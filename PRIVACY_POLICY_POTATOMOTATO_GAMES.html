Privacy Policy for PotatoMotato Games

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy covers every game published by PotatoMotato ("the Games"):

  • Sketch Circle (com.potatomotato.sketchcircle)
  • Combo Cards (com.potatomotato.combocards)
  • Alpha Ambush (com.potatomotato.alphaambush)
  • Home Racer (com.potatomotato.homeracer)
  • Pacmen (com.potatomotato.pacmen)
  • Chaotic Seas (com.potatomotato.chaoticseas)

The Games are local multiplayer party games. This policy explains how your information is handled.

2. Information Collection

2.1 What We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal Information: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (names, emails, addresses, phone numbers, etc.).
  • Server Data: The Games have no servers. Gameplay data, game state and player nicknames travel only between the devices playing together on your local network.
  • Advertising Data: The Games show no advertisements and collect no advertising information.
  • Tracking Data: The Games use no analytics or tracking services.
  • Location: The Games never read, store or transmit your location.

2.2 Device Permissions

The Games request the following permissions for gameplay only:

  • Bluetooth / Nearby devices: To find and connect to other players nearby. Connection data stays between the devices in the game.
  • Wi-Fi and network access: To connect players on the same local Wi-Fi network or a local hotspot. Game traffic stays on your local network.
  • Location: Older versions of Android require location permission before an app may scan for nearby Bluetooth or Wi-Fi devices. The Games use it for that scan only and never access your location.
  • Vibration: For haptic feedback during play.
  • Install packages / storage (older Android only): Used only when you choose "Share Game" to send the game directly to a friend's nearby device.
Important: All connectivity data is processed locally and never leaves the devices playing together.

3. In-App Purchases

  • Some Games offer an optional one-time purchase to unlock games with more players.
  • Purchases are handled by your device's app store (Google Play or the Apple App Store) and are subject to its privacy policy.
  • We never receive or store your payment information. Purchase status is kept on your device and by the app store.

4. Data Storage

  • Settings such as your nickname are stored locally on your device.
  • We maintain no servers, databases or cloud storage for game data.
  • Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything stored locally.

5. Third-Party Services

  • App stores: Purchases are subject to the privacy policy of Google or Apple.
  • No other third parties: The Games share no information with advertisers, analytics providers or any other third party.

6. Children's Privacy

The Games are suitable for families and do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Everything is processed locally on the device. If you have concerns about a child's privacy while using the Games, please contact us (Section 7).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Privacy Commitment Summary

  • No personal data collected
  • No servers; nothing leaves your local network
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking
  • Location permission used only for nearby-device scanning, never read
  • Optional one-time purchases handled by the app store
